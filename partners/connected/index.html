Add KeepIt to your Dock

In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu at the top right.

  1. Choose Cast, save and share.
  2. Choose Install page as app…, then Install.

KeepIt opens in its own window. Keep its icon in your Dock for quick access.

Use Chrome for access to your library folder. You may need to select the same folder when you first open the installed app.
